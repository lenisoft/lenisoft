## What I am building

### Jenga Review — know the place before you sign

A Kenya-first trust layer for renters, house agents, landlords, and buildings. The product turns scattered rental experiences into useful signal:

- Search real tenant experiences across buildings, estates, and house agents
- Give owners and managers a clear **Claim & verify** path
- Award a **Verified landlord** badge when evidence checks out
- Keep fake-review reports, agent complaints, and disputes in one calm moderation workspace
- Make the public experience feel optimistic, crisp, and human
